Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

HuisLaw.ai (“HuisLaw,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of information entrusted to our platform, particularly information associated with legal matters.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect information when you access or use HuisLaw.ai, the HuisLaw platform, client portals, applications, APIs, integrations, websites, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

HuisLaw is designed for attorneys, law firms, legal professionals, authorized staff, clients, experts, co-counsel, and other authorized participants in legal matters.

1. Customer Content and Legal Matter Data

“Customer Content” means information submitted to, connected to, generated within, or processed through the Services by or on behalf of a Customer or Authorized User.

Customer Content may include:

  • emails and email attachments;

  • documents and discovery materials;

  • client communications;

  • attorney notes and work product;

  • evidence and exhibits;

  • photographs, audio, and video;

  • transcripts;

  • court records and docket information;

  • financial records and invoices;

  • witness, expert, judge, opposing counsel, and contact information;

  • legal research;

  • prompts and AI conversations;

  • timelines;

  • case analysis;

  • information uploaded through client portals;

  • information obtained through authorized integrations;

  • other information associated with a legal matter.

Because of the nature of legal services, Customer Content may include attorney-client communications, attorney work product, confidential information, personally identifiable information, sensitive personal information, or information protected by law, court order, or professional responsibility rules.

For Customer Content submitted by or on behalf of a law firm or attorney, the law firm or attorney generally determines the purposes and means of processing that information. HuisLaw generally acts as a service provider or processor on the Customer’s behalf, subject to applicable law and contractual terms.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information such as:

Account Information. Name, business email address, organization, firm, role, location, authentication information, account settings, and user permissions.

Billing Information. Subscription information, billing address, transaction records, and payment-related information. Payment card information may be processed directly by our payment processor rather than stored by HuisLaw.

Customer Content. Information uploaded, connected, generated, transmitted, or processed through the Services.

Integration Information. Information received through services that a Customer chooses to connect, such as email providers, cloud-storage providers, practice-management systems, court systems, research platforms, or other software.

Technical Information. IP address, browser, device information, operating system, timestamps, login information, application activity, diagnostic information, API activity, and security events.

Usage Information. Features used, interactions with the Services, storage consumption, AI usage, and other operational information.

Support Information. Information provided when contacting HuisLaw for technical assistance, customer service, billing, procurement, or security support.

Website Information. Cookies, analytics, and similar information collected through HuisLaw.ai where those technologies are used.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • provide, maintain, operate, and secure the Services;

  • create and administer user, firm, client, and Matter accounts;

  • authenticate users and manage permissions;

  • ingest, organize, index, search, classify, and retrieve legal information;

  • analyze documents and evidence;

  • organize email and attachments;

  • build timelines and Matter intelligence;

  • generate AI-assisted research, summaries, drafts, analyses, and other requested outputs;

  • provide Case Outcome Intelligence and related analytical functions;

  • provide billing, subscription, and account administration;

  • provide technical and customer support;

  • maintain audit records and evidence provenance;

  • prevent fraud, misuse, unauthorized access, and security threats;

  • conduct backups, recovery, monitoring, and service maintenance;

  • comply with legal obligations;

  • enforce our agreements;

  • improve the reliability and usability of the Services using technical, aggregated, or appropriately de-identified information.

We do not use confidential Customer Content for unrelated advertising purposes.

4. Artificial Intelligence and Model Training

HuisLaw may use third-party artificial-intelligence providers and other subprocessors to provide AI-powered functions.

HuisLaw does not use Customer Content to train generalized AI models for other customers or for the public.

When third-party AI providers act as subprocessors for HuisLaw, we seek provider terms, configurations, and contractual protections intended to prevent the use of Customer Content to train generalized models.

AI may be used for functions including:

  • document analysis;

  • evidence organization;

  • email classification;

  • timeline generation;

  • research assistance;

  • drafting assistance;

  • claims and elements analysis;

  • contradiction detection;

  • deposition and discovery assistance;

  • damages analysis;

  • Matter Brain functions;

  • Case Outcome Intelligence.

Artificial intelligence can make mistakes. AI-generated output may be incomplete, inaccurate, outdated, or inappropriate for a particular legal matter.

Attorneys and other qualified legal professionals remain responsible for reviewing and verifying AI-generated information before relying on it.

5. Customer Ownership

The law firm or Customer retains ownership of its Customer Content.

HuisLaw does not claim ownership of Customer Content merely because the information is uploaded to, processed by, analyzed by, or generated within the platform.

Customers grant HuisLaw only the limited rights reasonably necessary to host, store, copy, transmit, index, process, analyze, transform, secure, back up, and otherwise process Customer Content to provide the Services, comply with authorized Customer instructions, maintain security, or comply with law.

6. Disclosure of Information

HuisLaw does not sell Customer Content.

We may disclose information to:

Service Providers and Subprocessors. Providers that support hosting, storage, artificial intelligence, authentication, payments, communications, monitoring, security, analytics, customer support, and other infrastructure.

Customer-Authorized Integrations. Services that a Customer chooses to connect to HuisLaw.

Legal Authorities. When disclosure is required by applicable law, valid legal process, subpoena, court order, regulation, or governmental request.

Security and Protection. When reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, abuse, security threats, unauthorized access, or threats to users or the Services.

Business Transactions. In connection with a financing, merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of business assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal protections.

HuisLaw does not intentionally disclose one Customer’s confidential Customer Content to another Customer without authorization.

7. Attorney-Client Privilege, Work Product, and Confidentiality

HuisLaw is a technology provider and is not a law firm.

The Services are designed to assist Customers in protecting and organizing sensitive legal information, including information that a Customer may designate as:

  • attorney-client privileged;

  • attorney work product;

  • confidential;

  • highly confidential;

  • internal firm only;

  • subject to a protective order;

  • otherwise restricted.

A designation inside HuisLaw is an organizational and access-control feature. Merely placing information in a privileged or restricted folder does not itself create attorney-client privilege, work-product protection, or another legal protection.

Customers remain responsible for determining the legal status of their information and configuring appropriate permissions.

If authorized HuisLaw personnel must access Customer Content for support, security, maintenance, or legal compliance, access should be limited to the minimum reasonably necessary and subject to applicable confidentiality obligations.

8. Security

HuisLaw maintains a security program designed to protect information against unauthorized access, destruction, loss, misuse, alteration, and disclosure.

Security controls may include:

  • encryption in transit;

  • encryption at rest;

  • access controls;

  • Matter-level permissions;

  • authentication safeguards;

  • logging and monitoring;

  • backup and recovery processes;

  • secure development practices;

  • vulnerability management;

  • incident-response procedures.

No information system is completely secure, and HuisLaw cannot guarantee absolute security.

More information is available on our Security, Confidentiality & Responsible AI page.

9. Evidence Integrity

HuisLaw may provide functionality designed to preserve original evidence while allowing attorneys and authorized users to create working copies, annotations, redactions, or derivative versions.

Evidence-management features may include file hashes, timestamps, version history, upload information, access history, provenance records, duplicate detection, and chain-of-custody information.

These tools assist legal professionals but do not independently determine whether evidence is authentic, admissible, or legally sufficient.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • provide the Services;

  • satisfy contractual obligations;

  • maintain security and audit records;

  • comply with applicable law;

  • honor legal holds;

  • resolve disputes;

  • enforce agreements.

Customers should export information they need before terminating their account where applicable.

Subject to contractual obligations, legal holds, backup schedules, security requirements, and applicable law, Customer Content may be deleted following account termination or an authorized deletion request.

  • request access;

  • request deletion;

  • obtain a copy of certain information;

Such information is treated as Customer Content and subject to the applicable Matter permissions and contractual protections.

HuisLaw is based in the United States.

Information may be processed in the United States or other jurisdictions where approved service providers operate. Where required, appropriate contractual or legal safeguards will be used for cross-border processing.
