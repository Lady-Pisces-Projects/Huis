Security & privilege

Privilege comes first. Always.

Huis was designed around attorney-client privilege and work-product protection from the first line of code.

Encryption

Evidence is encrypted in storage and in transit, and scanned before it enters a matter.

Ethical walls

Matter-level access and role-based permissions keep walled matters out of reach, even from search and AI.

Audit trail & provenance

Chain of custody for every item, original files preserved, and a record of who did what.

No training on your data

Client material is never used to train AI models.

AI disclaimer. Huis is a tool for attorneys. It does not provide legal advice and does not replace attorney judgment. AI-assisted outputs cite their sources and should be verified by a licensed attorney before they are relied on.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does Huis replace my judgment as an attorney?

No. Huis organizes the matter and shows you what the evidence says, with sources. Strategy and legal conclusions stay with you.

Who can see a matter's information?

Only people your firm gives access to that matter. Ethical walls apply everywhere in Huis, including search and the Matter Brain.

Is my client data used to train AI?

No. Client material is never used to train AI models.

What happens when my trial ends?

Choose monthly or yearly to keep going. If your firm hasn't made its first payment by the end of the trial, adding new work is suspended, and for at least 30 days everyone at the firm can still sign in, view and export every matter. We email the firm's owners 14 days, 7 days and 1 day before the deletion date. On that date the firm's account and all of its data are permanently deleted and can't be restored.

Your firm is solely responsible for exporting and keeping anything it needs before then, including records it must retain under the rules of professional conduct, court rules or a litigation hold. To the fullest extent permitted by law, Huis Law is not responsible for any loss arising from deleting trial data under these terms. Once your firm has made its first payment, Huis never deletes its cases for non-payment.
