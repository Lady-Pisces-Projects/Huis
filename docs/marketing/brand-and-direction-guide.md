# Huis Law — Brand Palette & Direction Guide

Status: confirmed direction (November 2026). Funnel updated 10 November 2026: self-serve, video-led, 7-day free trial (replaces the demo-request / pilot funnel).

## Brand hierarchy

**HUIS LAW**
**Your Case Has a Story. Huis Helps You See It.**
*Organize the evidence. Connect the facts. Find the gaps. Build the strategy.*

- Brand line (under logo): **Connected case intelligence.**
- Core promise: **Huis Law transforms scattered case information into organized legal intelligence.**
- Long-form version: Huis Law connects the evidence, communications, people, dates, and legal issues surrounding a matter so attorneys can understand the case faster, identify what matters, and determine what needs attention next.
- Positioning: not "AI for lawyers" (too broad). Lead with getting attorneys organized across their caseload: case organization, evidence intelligence, faster case understanding, and better preparation.

## Logo & type

- Mark: gold column-style "H" monogram (pillars on each side, crossbar).
- Lockup: "HUIS LAW" in wide-tracked serif capitals, "Connected case intelligence." beneath.
- Website display face: serif (closest Google Fonts match: Libre Caslon Text). App: system sans.
- Imagery: dark navy, warm gold light, law library, scales of justice, laptop showing the Matter Brain. No emoji in product.

## Named products

- **Huis Matter Brain™**: the intelligence layer for each matter. Ask: "What evidence supports this claim?", "Where does this witness contradict their earlier statement?", "What happened between March 12 and April 4?", "Which elements still lack supporting evidence?", "Show me every communication involving this person."
- **Case Outcome Intelligence™**: premium feature, not the entry offer. Evaluates evidence strength, claims/defenses, legal elements, missing evidence, contradictions, damages documentation, witnesses, chronology, case law, jurisdiction, court and judge context. Framing: does not replace attorney judgment; gives better visibility before strategic decisions.

## What Huis does (feature list)

- Organize case evidence automatically
- Connect documents and communications to the correct matter
- Build intelligent case timelines
- Identify inconsistencies and contradictions
- Find missing evidence or unanswered questions
- Map evidence to claims and legal elements
- Search the entire matter conversationally
- Profiles for witnesses, experts, opposing counsel, judges, contacts
- Preserve original evidence while creating AI-assisted working intelligence

Built for legal work: attorney-client privilege, work-product protection, role-based permissions, evidence provenance, matter-level access, secure client portals, audit trails, original-file preservation.

## Funnel (decided 10 Nov 2026: self-serve)

Goal: sell **monthly or yearly subscriptions**. No demo calls; the explainer video is the demo.

Traffic (LinkedIn / Google / attorney outreach / conferences / referrals)
→ Landing page with the **product explainer video** up top
→ CTA: **Start Your 7-Day Free Trial**
→ Short sign-up (name, firm, email, practice area, number of attorneys)
→ In-app onboarding: connect Outlook or Gmail, upload one case, see the Matter Brain work (a sample matter with made-up data is ready for anyone who wants to look before using real files)
→ Trial reminder emails (day 1, 3, 5, 6, 7)
→ Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly** (yearly at a discount)
→ Premium upgrade later: Case Outcome Intelligence

Pricing: $237/user/month; yearly price to be set; enterprise pricing for larger firms.

Trust work the video and page must carry, since no salesperson is in the loop: a security and privilege section (encryption, ethical walls, audit trail, no training on client data), a link to the FAQ, and the AI disclaimer.
